Privacy Policy – Choghadiya Panchang (ચોઘડિયા પંચાંગ)

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · App: Choghadiya Panchang (package com.kbsc.choghadiya)

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device information. It works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. It does not request any Android permission: it has no access to the internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, SMS or phone.

Information kept on your device

The app saves only your own settings on your phone: the language (Gujarati or English), the place you selected (city name and latitude/longitude) and the time format. These stay in the app's private storage on your device, are not sent anywhere, are excluded from cloud backup, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

How the app works

All Choghadiya, Panchang and festival timings are calculated on your device from astronomical formulas. No data is downloaded or uploaded.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
